# UniBox 📦

UniBox is a **Universal Smart Inbox** that solves digital hoarding across fragmented apps. You share content from *any* app to UniBox via the native Android Share Sheet, and it becomes a single, searchable, categorized inbox.

### 📚 Documentation
- [[Features]] - Overview of what the app can do
- [[Architecture]] - Clean Architecture and UDF
- [[Database]] - Room, SQLite, and FTS4 Full-Text Search
- [[Background Tasks]] - WorkManager and JSoup OG scraping
- [[Machine Learning]] - ML Kit OCR Text Recognition
- [[Geofencing]] - Location-based reminders via Play Services

### 🚀 Tech Stack
* **UI**: Jetpack Compose (Material 3)
* **Architecture**: MVVM, Clean Architecture, Unidirectional Data Flow (UDF)
* **Concurrency**: Kotlin Coroutines & Flow
* **DI**: Dagger Hilt
* **Persistence**: Room Database
* **Background Work**: WorkManager
* **Machine Learning**: Google ML Kit (Text Recognition)
* **Location**: Google Play Services (Location & Geofencing)
